Short bio – Louis Mesny

Associate Professor, ISAE‑SUPAERO
Department of Mechanics of Structures and Materials
Researcher, Institut Clément Ader

Louis Mesny completed his PhD in 2024 on hybrid nonlinear vibration absorbers in structural mechanics. He is now an associate professor at ISAE‑SUPAERO in Toulouse. His research focuses on nonlinear dynamics and active control of structures, with a particular interest in vibration phenomena. He works on understanding and reducing vibrations in complex mechanical systems. His approach combines theoretical modelling, numerical simulations, and experimental methods in mechanics.
